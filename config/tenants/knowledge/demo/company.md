# Demo Store - Company Knowledge

This folder is the demo tenant's knowledge base. Every .md/.txt file here is
chunked by paragraph and searched by the `search_company_kb` tool whenever a
caller asks something company-specific. Edit these files freely - changes
apply to the next call, no restart needed. Replace this content with your own
company's information (use one heading per topic, short paragraphs).

## Opening hours

The Demo Store support line is open Monday to Friday from 9 AM to 7 PM IST,
and Saturday from 10 AM to 2 PM. We are closed on Sundays and public
holidays. During closed hours the AI assistant can take a message and
schedule a callback for the next working day.

## Return and refund policy

Customers can return any unused item within 30 days of delivery for a full
refund. Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5 to 7
business days after the returned item reaches our warehouse. Damaged or
wrong items can be reported within 48 hours of delivery for an immediate
replacement at no cost.

## Shipping

We ship to all serviceable pin codes across India. Standard delivery takes 3
to 5 working days and is free on orders above Rs 999; below that a Rs 49
shipping fee applies. Express delivery (1 to 2 working days) costs Rs 149.
Every order gets a tracking link by SMS and WhatsApp once it ships.

## Pricing and plans

The Demo Store loyalty program has three tiers: Silver, Gold and Platinum.
Silver is free for everyone. Gold costs Rs 499 per year and adds free
express shipping. Platinum costs Rs 1,499 per year and adds early access to
sales and a dedicated support line.

## Store locations

Our flagship store is in Connaught Place, New Delhi, open the same hours as
the support line. A second store operates in Koramangala, Bangalore.

## Contact and escalation

For anything the assistant cannot resolve, callers can request a human
callback; our team returns calls within 2 working hours. The written
complaints channel is care@demostore.example and replies come within one
business day.
